It does not relicense third-party material. In particular:

- Government Procurement Policy Board restriction and CPES records remain
  official GPPB publications. Busisi archives source fingerprints and applies
  its own identity, quarantine, and presentation rules.
- Self-hosted fonts retain the licenses identified in
  `cloudflare/busisi/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and their bundled license files.
- The Busisi and Auva Labs names, logos, and marks are not granted for use as
  trademarks. A modified deployment must not imply endorsement or official
  status and should replace the Busisi brand assets.

The repository does not commit the generated acquisition archive, publication
database, review queue, or source-image collection. Factual hashes, counts, and
provenance statements in dated snapshots are published to support verification
and do not alter the terms of the underlying source material.

When republishing a Busisi-derived result, identify the original publisher,
the access route when relevant, the Busisi processing step, and the dated
snapshot or commit used. Do not present a review signal as a finding.
