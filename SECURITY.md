# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability, exposed credential,
private infrastructure detail, or route that could change or destroy data.

Send a concise report to [dev@auvalabs.com](mailto:dev@auvalabs.com) with:

- the affected route, file, or component;
- reproduction steps and expected impact;
- whether the issue is already public; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not access records beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue. Do
not alter production data, degrade availability, or publish private material.

Auva Labs will acknowledge a complete report, assess its severity, and
coordinate remediation and disclosure. No reward or response deadline is
promised.

## Supported version

Only the current `main` branch and the production deployment at
<https://busisi.org/> are supported. Historical commits and independent forks
are not maintained by Auva Labs.

Data corrections and source disputes are not security vulnerabilities. Submit
those through the data-correction issue form or email
[dev@auvalabs.com](mailto:dev@auvalabs.com).
