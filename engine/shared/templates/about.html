About {{ vertical.display_name }}

A real-time knowledge platform for the AI/ML field — news the day it breaks, evergreen reference for the concepts that don't, original research drawn from accumulated coverage, and tools that make the field's economics legible.

What this is

{{ vertical.display_name }} aggregates first-party announcements from AI labs, research preprints, regulatory filings, and the work of independent voices. It clusters parallel coverage of a single event so the front page reads like a curator's desk, not a press-release feed. Layered on top: a glossary that defines every term you'd hit, beginner's guides, comparisons, and decision tools.

Who it's for

Practitioners (researchers, engineers, product builders), decision-makers (founders, executives, policy people), and serious students. Skim-friendly when you have ten minutes; deep when you want it.

What's different

Two things. First, the editorial discipline: every story links back to its source prominently, AI-generated summaries are explicitly grounded in those sources, and analysis is marked separately from fact reporting. Second, the architecture: the same engine that powers the front page also generates the reference layer, which keeps the two pegged to each other — the glossary cross-links into every story that uses a term.

How to use it

Read the front page for the day's signal. Subscribe to the daily digest if you'd rather have it delivered. Search for a term? The glossary is exhaustive. Comparing models? The tools section has live comparisons.

Who runs it

{{ vertical.display_name }} is operated by Auva Labs FZE. The operating entity is identified on /privacy and /terms. Editorial methodology is documented at /methodology — including the AI tools we use, how we ground summaries, and what we won't do.
