Rules before results

Methodology

The system is deterministic and evidence-first. It can operate without a language model, and every published conclusion must be reproducible from its inputs.

Evidence pipeline Identity matching Review signals Release gate
01

Evidence pipeline

Sources are acquired, hashed, archived, schema-validated, normalized to exact pesos and centavos, and reconciled to source control totals before analysis.

  1. Acquire and archive original bytes
  2. Validate schema and units
  3. Reconcile control totals
  4. Match identities
  5. Classify confidence
  6. Calculate signals
  7. Quarantine ambiguity
  8. Publish reviewed evidence
02

Identity matching

An exact link requires a shared official identifier. A derived link requires a unique combination of year, title, location, funding source, and compatible amount. Competing candidates are quarantined rather than resolved by picking the highest score.

03

Review signals

Each signal exposes its rule, inputs, calculation, confidence, and limitations. An insertion candidate, repeated appropriation, amount difference, or delayed source update is a lead for review. It is not evidence of illegality or wrongdoing.
