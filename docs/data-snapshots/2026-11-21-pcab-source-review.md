# PCAB source review, 21 November 2026

## Direct answer

The official PCAB regular-license grid fell from 18,604 to 18,560 records. Busisi
did not accept that decrease automatically. The saved responses were compared
record by record, and six records received an additional check against their
official license-detail pages.

The change is safe to monitor with a revised minimum only because the official
detail pages still identify the five active licenses omitted from the grid and
confirm the changed record as a renewal. These detail pages must continue to
pass on every source check.

## Reviewed minimum

The new grid minimum is 18,422 records. It starts from the previously reviewed
18,604 records and subtracts the 177 expired removals and five active records
now checked separately through official detail pages. The 138 additions do not
increase the safety margin.

Any future response below 18,422 records fails. A missing or changed required
detail record also fails. New active omissions or unexplained retained-record
changes require another archived, record-level review.

## What this does not prove

This review verifies the current PCAB source transition. It does not prove that
a contractor performed public work correctly, that a contract was properly
awarded, or that a license was valid on every earlier project date.
