# Community conduct

Busisi discussions must remain useful to people examining public evidence.

Participants are expected to:

- address evidence and methods rather than personal or political identity;
- provide sources for factual challenges;
- distinguish questions, review signals, and established findings;
- accept corrections when stronger evidence is provided;
- protect personal information and report security concerns privately; and
- communicate without harassment, threats, discrimination, or intimidation.

Unacceptable behavior includes targeted abuse, publishing private information,
coordinated disruption, knowingly false evidence, partisan campaigning in
project channels, or pressure to change a result outside the published rules.

Auva Labs may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
standards and may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct issues
to [dev@auvalabs.com](mailto:dev@auvalabs.com). Reports will be reviewed as
privately as practical, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
